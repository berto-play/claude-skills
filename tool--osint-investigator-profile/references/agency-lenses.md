# Nine-Agency Lens Analysis

Optional module. Operator activates specific lenses by name. Not all lenses are relevant to every target.

Each lens produces a structured output: **Interest level** (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH) + reasoning + the specific exposure or signal that drives it.

Available lenses — activate by name:

| Lens | Frame | Activate when |
|---|---|---|
| `FBI` | Domestic US financial-crime nexus, wire fraud, money laundering, sanctions evasion | Target has US operations, US investors, or dollar-denominated transactions |
| `CIA` | Foreign influence, dual-use technology, counterintelligence flag | Target operates in tech, defence-adjacent, or dual-citizenship context |
| `FSB/SVR` | Russian capital, kompromat exposure, critical infrastructure | Target has Russian shareholders, advisors, or operations |
| `Mossad` | Middle East regional ties, Lebanese/Israeli linkages, dual-citizenship patterns | Target has Lebanese, Israeli, or MENA-regional exposure |
| `MSS` | Chinese technology transfer, BRI exposure, Chinese capital | Target has Chinese investors, IP licensing, or hardware supply |
| `BND/DGSE/AISE` | EU member-state capital flows (Germany/France/Italy) | Target operates across EU jurisdictions with public-sector adjacent activity |
| `Regulator` | Consob, FCA, SEC, BaFin compliance-grade exposure | Target is in financial services, fundraising, or regulated industry |
| `Journalist` | OCCRP/ICIJ angle: would this story land in a leaks consortium | Target has opacity, offshore structures, or political exposure |
| `Adversary` | What a competitor or hostile counterparty could do with target's exposure | Always useful; produces a threat surface map |
