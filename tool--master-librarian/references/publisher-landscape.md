# Publisher Landscape

### Academic Presses: Prestige and Specialization

**Top-tier university presses (US)**
- MIT Press: technology, cognitive science, 
  architecture, critical theory, economics
- Harvard University Press: history, philosophy, 
  economics, science
- Princeton University Press: mathematics, physics, 
  political science, economics
- University of Chicago Press: sociology, economics, 
  history, philosophy, linguistics
- Stanford University Press: technology, Asian studies, 
  law
- Yale University Press: history, art history, 
  political philosophy
- Columbia University Press: philosophy, East Asian 
  studies, media studies
- Cornell University Press: labor, history, 
  international studies
- University of California Press: social sciences, 
  environmental humanities

**UK university presses**
- Oxford University Press: reference, philosophy, 
  theology, law, history.
- Cambridge University Press: sciences, humanities, 
  social sciences.
- Edinburgh University Press: philosophy, linguistics, 
  film studies
- Manchester University Press: political science, 
  history

**Quality within university presses**
All university presses use peer review, but prestige 
varies within a press by series. Series editors matter: 
check who edits a series when assessing a book in it.

### Major Commercial Academic Publishers

- Elsevier: the largest. Science Direct is their 
  platform. Research universities pay $2-10 million 
  annually for journal subscriptions. This pricing 
  structure is why open access and shadow libraries 
  exist.
- Springer Nature: Springer + Nature + Macmillan
- Wiley: broad across disciplines
- Taylor and Francis / Routledge: humanities and 
  social sciences dominant
- Sage: social sciences
- JSTOR: aggregator, not publisher, but essential 
  access platform

**Open Access Models**
- Gold OA: free to read, author pays article 
  processing charge (APC)
- Diamond OA: free to read, free to publish, 
  funded by institutions or societies
- Green OA: author deposits accepted manuscript 
  in repository after embargo period
- Hybrid: journal charges subscriptions but 
  allows individual articles to be made OA 
  for a fee

**Transformative agreements**: many institutions now 
negotiate "read and publish" deals that bundle 
subscription access with OA publishing rights. 
Check your institution's agreements before paying 
an APC.

**Author rights**: authors often retain the right 
to post accepted manuscripts in repositories even 
when the final version is paywalled. Check 
SHERPA/RoMEO (sherpa.ac.uk/romeo) for journal 
policies on self-archiving.

### Open Access Publishers

**Gold open access**
- PLOS: biology, medicine, genetics
- eLife: biology and medicine, rigorous
- F1000Research: life sciences, open peer review
- Frontiers: broad, quality varies, check individual 
  journals
- PeerJ: biology and computer science
- MDPI: broad, quality varies significantly

**Diamond open access**
- Many society journals operate this way
- Episciences: mathematics and computer science
- Discrete Analysis: mathematics

**Preprint servers (not peer-reviewed)**
- arXiv: physics, math, CS, economics
- bioRxiv / medRxiv: biology, medicine
- SSRN: social sciences
- PhilPapers / PhilArchive: philosophy

### Predatory Publishers

Publish for fees without rigorous review. A serious 
problem in evaluating sources.

**How to identify**
- Think. Check. Submit. (thinkchecksubmit.org): 
  the current gold standard checklist for evaluating 
  a journal before submitting or citing
- DOAJ (Directory of Open Access Journals): 
  legitimate journals are listed; check before citing
- Cabells Predatory Reports: subscription service 
  tracking predatory journals

Note: Beall's List was the original predatory 
publisher resource but has not been actively 
maintained since 2017. Use the above tools instead.

**Red flags**
- Journal scope does not match its claimed field
- Unsolicited email invitations to publish
- Suspiciously fast submission-to-acceptance time
- No real evidence of peer review
- Copied design from legitimate journals
- High article processing charges with no selectivity

### Retractions and Corrections

**Retraction Watch (retractionwatch.com):**
Tracks retracted and corrected papers. Essential 
for contested fields.

**High-retraction areas (check carefully):**
- Nutrition research
- Psychology (replication crisis)
- Cancer biology
- Social priming research

**How to check:**
Search Retraction Watch database before citing any 
high-stakes paper in a contested area.

### Trade Publishers for Serious Nonfiction

Not peer-reviewed but often publish important work:

- Farrar, Straus and Giroux: literary nonfiction
- Penguin Press and Allen Lane: serious nonfiction
- Basic Books: science, social science
- W.W. Norton: history, memoir, science
- Public Affairs: politics and policy
- The New Press: progressive politics and social issues
- Verso: left-leaning theory and politics
- Semiotext(e): critical theory
