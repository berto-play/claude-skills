# Emerging and Interdisciplinary Fields

### Cognitive Science

The intersection of psychology, neuroscience, linguistics, 
philosophy of mind, computer science, and anthropology.

**Foundational**
- Gardner: The Mind's New Science.
- Fodor: The Modularity of Mind.
- Minsky: The Society of Mind.

**Embodied and Extended Cognition**
- Varela, Thompson and Rosch: The Embodied Mind.
- Clark: Being There and Supersizing the Mind.
- Clark and Chalmers: "The Extended Mind" (essay, 1998).
- Lakoff and Johnson: Philosophy in the Flesh.

**Key Journals**
- Cognitive Science
- Cognition
- Trends in Cognitive Sciences
- Behavioral and Brain Sciences

### Complexity Science and Systems Thinking

**Foundational**
- Wiener: Cybernetics (1948). The feedback concept.
- Bertalanffy: General System Theory.
- Ashby: An Introduction to Cybernetics (free online)
- Prigogine and Stengers: Order Out of Chaos.

**Modern Complexity**
- Holland: Hidden Order.
- Kauffman: The Origins of Order.
- Strogatz: Sync and Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos.
- Barabasi: Linked.
- Watts: Six Degrees and Small Worlds.
- Mitchell: Complexity: A Guided Tour. Best accessible 
  introduction.
- Bar-Yam: Making Things Work. Accessible systems 
  thinking for real-world problems.

**Santa Fe Institute**
Primary research center for complexity science. 
Papers and working papers at santafe.edu. 
SFI Press publishes open-access books.

### Artificial Intelligence (Current)

This field moves faster than any other. Preprints are 
the primary literature. Any specific claim about 
capabilities or state of the art should be verified 
against recent arXiv papers; do not rely on training 
knowledge for this domain.

**Conceptual Foundations**
- Turing: "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (1950)
- McCarthy et al.: Dartmouth Proposal (1955).
- Minsky and Papert: Perceptrons.
- Rumelhart et al.: Parallel Distributed Processing (1986).

**Deep Learning Era (2012 onwards)**
- Krizhevsky, Sutskever and Hinton: AlexNet (NeurIPS 2012).
- Goodfellow et al.: GANs (2014)
- Vaswani et al.: "Attention Is All You Need" (2017).
- Brown et al.: GPT-3 (2020)
- Wei et al.: "Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models" 
  (2022)

**Current Sources**
- arXiv cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.CL: primary literature
- Papers with Code: paperwithcode.com
- Distill.pub: deep learning explained
- Alignment Forum: AI safety research

**AI Safety and Ethics**
- Russell: Human Compatible
- Bostrom: Superintelligence
- Christian: The Alignment Problem
- Gabriel: "Artificial Intelligence, Values, and Alignment" 
  (Minds and Machines, 2020)

### Science and Technology Studies (STS)

Examines how science, technology, and society 
co-constitute each other.

**Foundational**
- Kuhn: The Structure of Scientific Revolutions
- Latour and Woolgar: Laboratory Life.
- Latour: Science in Action.
- Pinch and Bijker: "The Social Construction of Facts 
  and Artifacts" (essay, 1984).
- Winner: "Do Artifacts Have Politics?" (essay, 1980).
- Jasanoff: States of Knowledge.

**Key Journals**
- Social Studies of Science
- Science, Technology and Human Values
- Technology and Culture

### Data Science and Statistics

An increasingly essential interdisciplinary field. 
Spans frequentist and Bayesian inference, causal 
reasoning, and machine learning.

**Statistical Foundations**
- Jaynes: Probability Theory: The Logic of Science. 
  Bayesian framework, rigorous.
- Gelman et al.: Bayesian Data Analysis (3rd ed). 
  Standard graduate reference.
- McElreath: Statistical Rethinking. 
  Best accessible Bayesian introduction.
- Cox and Hinkley: Theoretical Statistics. 
  Frequentist foundation.
- Efron and Hastie: Computer Age Statistical Inference. 
  Free online.

**Causal Inference**
- Pearl: Causality. The foundational framework.
- Pearl and Mackenzie: The Book of Why (accessible)
- Rubin: "Estimating Causal Effects of Treatments" 
  (1974 paper). The potential outcomes framework.
- Angrist and Pischke: Mostly Harmless Econometrics. 
  Applied causal inference.
- Hernán and Robins: Causal Inference: What If. 
  Free online.

**Machine Learning**
- Hastie, Tibshirani and Friedman: The Elements of 
  Statistical Learning (ESL). Free online.
- James et al.: An Introduction to Statistical Learning 
  (ISL). More accessible, free online.

**Key Journals**
- Journal of the American Statistical Association (JASA)
- Journal of the Royal Statistical Society
- Biometrika
- Journal of Machine Learning Research (JMLR, open access)
- arXiv stat.ML and stat.ME

**Data Repositories**
- ICPSR: social science datasets
- Zenodo: open research data
- Harvard Dataverse
- OSF: Open Science Framework
- UCI Machine Learning Repository
