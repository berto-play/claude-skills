# Applied Fields: Intellectual Topology

### Medicine and Public Health

**Historical**
- Hippocratic Corpus (5th-4th century BCE).
- Vesalius: De Humani Corporis Fabrica (1543).
- Harvey: De Motu Cordis (1628).
- Snow: On the Mode of Communication of Cholera (1855). 
  The birth of epidemiology.
- Semmelweis: Etiology of Childbed Fever.
- Pasteur and Koch: Germ theory papers (1860s-1880s).

**Textbook Canon**
- Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine.
- Robbins and Cotran: Pathologic Basis of Disease
- Gray's Anatomy
- Goodman and Gilman: The Pharmacological Basis of 
  Therapeutics

**Evidence-Based Medicine**
- Sackett et al.: Clinical Epidemiology.
- Ioannidis: "Why Most Published Research Findings Are 
  False" (PLOS Medicine, 2005). Essential for evaluating 
  medical claims.
- Cochrane Reviews: gold standard systematic reviews at 
  cochranelibrary.com. Many free.

**Public Health**
- Marmot: The Status Syndrome and Social Determinants 
  of Health (WHO report).
- Rose: The Strategy of Preventive Medicine.

**Key Journals**
- New England Journal of Medicine
- The Lancet
- JAMA
- BMJ (much open access)
- PubMed Central: free access hub
- Cochrane Library

### Law

**Historical and Philosophical Foundations**
- Justinian: Corpus Juris Civilis.
- Blackstone: Commentaries on the Laws of England.
- Austin: The Province of Jurisprudence Determined.
- Holmes: The Common Law.
- Kelsen: Pure Theory of Law.
- Hart: The Concept of Law.
- Dworkin: Law's Empire.
- Fuller: The Morality of Law.

**International Law**
- Grotius: The Law of War and Peace (1625).
- Crawford (formerly Brownlie): Principles of Public 
  International Law.

**Access**
- PACER: US federal courts
- BAILII: British and Irish free legal access
- CanLII: Canadian free legal access
- EUR-Lex: European Union law, free
- CourtListener (courtlistener.com): free US case law
- HeinOnline: law reviews and historical legal materials

**Key Journals**
- Harvard Law Review
- Yale Law Journal
- Columbia Law Review
- Modern Law Review (UK)
- American Journal of International Law

### Computer Science

**Historical**
- Turing: "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (1950).
- Shannon: "A Mathematical Theory of Communication" (1948).
- von Neumann: First Draft of a Report on the EDVAC (1945).
- Dijkstra: "Go To Statement Considered Harmful" (1968).
- Knuth: The Art of Computer Programming (4+ vols).

**Foundational Texts**
- SICP: Abelson and Sussman. Free online at MIT.
- CLRS: Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein
- Sipser: Introduction to the Theory of Computation
- Patterson and Hennessy: Computer Organization and Design
- Tanenbaum: Modern Operating Systems
- Kurose and Ross: Computer Networking

**AI and Machine Learning**
- Russell and Norvig: Artificial Intelligence: A Modern 
  Approach
- Bishop: Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning
- Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville: Deep Learning 
  (free online)
- Sutton and Barto: Reinforcement Learning (free online)
- Key papers: attention mechanism (Vaswani et al., 2017), 
  AlexNet (2012), AlphaGo (2016), GPT series

**Key Resources**
- arXiv (cs section): essential for current AI research
- Papers with Code: papers with implementations
- ACM Digital Library
- IEEE Xplore

**Key Venues (conferences matter more than journals)**
- NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR: machine learning
- CVPR, ICCV, ECCV: computer vision
- ACL, EMNLP, NAACL: natural language processing
- STOC, FOCS: theory of computing
- SOSP, OSDI: systems
- CCS, IEEE S&P, USENIX Security: security
