# Natural Sciences: Intellectual Topology

### Physics

**Foundational Texts (Historical)**
- Newton: Principia Mathematica (1687). Read Chandrasekhar's 
  Newton's Principia for the Common Reader as a guide.
- Faraday and Maxwell: Faraday's Experimental Researches in 
  Electricity, Maxwell's Treatise on Electricity and 
  Magnetism. The field concept is born here.
- Boltzmann: Lectures on Gas Theory.
- Einstein: The original 1905 papers (special relativity, 
  photoelectric effect, Brownian motion). All in Annalen 
  der Physik, all publicly accessible now.
- Bohr, Heisenberg, Schrödinger, Dirac: The founding papers 
  of quantum mechanics (1920s). Collected in Sources of 
  Quantum Mechanics (van der Waerden, ed.).

**Modern Textbook Canon**
- Classical mechanics: Goldstein, Poole and Safko
- Electrodynamics: Griffiths (undergraduate), 
  Jackson (graduate)
- Quantum mechanics: Griffiths (undergraduate), 
  Sakurai (graduate)
- Statistical mechanics: Kittel and Kroemer (undergraduate), 
  Pathria (graduate)
- General relativity: Carroll (accessible), 
  Misner, Thorne and Wheeler (comprehensive)
- Particle physics: Griffiths Introduction to Elementary 
  Particles

**Current Frontiers**
- Quantum gravity: Loop quantum gravity (Rovelli) vs. 
  string theory (Polchinski). Unresolved.
- Dark matter and dark energy: No confirmed detection of 
  dark matter particle. ΛCDM model standard but contested.
- Quantum information and quantum computing: 
  Nielsen and Chuang is the standard text.
- Condensed matter: increasingly where much fundamental 
  physics is happening.

**Key Journals**
- Physical Review Letters
- Physical Review A/B/C/D/E: specialized by subfield
- Nature Physics, Science: landmark results
- Journal of High Energy Physics: open access
- arXiv (hep-th, hep-ph, gr-qc, quant-ph, cond-mat)

**Shadow Curriculum**
- Feynman Lectures on Physics (3 vols): the best 
  exposition of classical and quantum physics ever written
- Landau and Lifshitz Course of Theoretical Physics (10 vols)
- Zee: Quantum Field Theory in a Nutshell
- Penrose: The Road to Reality

### Mathematics

**Essential for navigating scientific literature**
- Calculus and analysis: the language of change
- Linear algebra: the language of states and transformations
- Probability and statistics: the language of uncertainty
- Differential equations: the language of dynamics
- Topology: the language of shape and continuity
- Abstract algebra: the language of structure

**Best accessible texts**
- Strogatz: Infinite Powers (calculus, narrative)
- Strang: Linear Algebra and Its Applications
- Jaynes: Probability Theory: The Logic of Science (Bayesian)
- Strogatz: Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos
- Munkres: Topology (standard graduate text)

### Chemistry

**Historical**
- Lavoisier: Elements of Chemistry (1789).
- Mendeleev: Original periodic table papers (1869)
- Lewis: The Atom and the Molecule (1916).
- Pauling: The Nature of the Chemical Bond.

**Modern Canon**
- Physical chemistry: Atkins' Physical Chemistry
- Organic: Clayden, Greeves and Warren
- Inorganic: Shriver and Atkins
- Biochemistry: Stryer (now Berg, Tymoczko, Stryer)
- Analytical: Skoog, West, Holler

**Current Frontiers**
- Synthetic biology (overlaps with biology)
- Materials chemistry and battery science
- Computational chemistry and molecular dynamics
- Green chemistry and sustainable synthesis
- Protein folding (AlphaFold changed this field)

**Key Journals**
- Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS)
- Angewandte Chemie
- Nature Chemistry
- Chemical Science (Royal Society of Chemistry, open access)
- Chemical Reviews: essential for literature surveys

### Biology

**Historical**
- Darwin: On the Origin of Species (1859). Read the first 
  edition, not abridgments.
- Mendel: Experiments on Plant Hybridization (1866).
- Watson and Crick: original 1953 Nature paper (1 page). 
  Also read Franklin's contribution in the same issue.
- Jacob and Monod: papers on the lac operon and gene 
  regulation (1961).

**Modern Canon**
- Cell biology: Alberts' Molecular Biology of the Cell
- Genetics: Lewin's Genes
- Evolution: Futuyma's Evolution, then Dawkins' 
  The Selfish Gene (popular but important)
- Ecology: Begon, Townsend and Harper's Ecology
- Molecular biology: Watson's Molecular Biology of the Gene
- Neuroscience: Kandel et al. Principles of Neural Science

**Current Frontiers**
- CRISPR and gene editing: Doudna and Charpentier's 
  original 2012 Science paper
- Single-cell genomics
- Protein structure: AlphaFold2 paper (Jumper et al., 
  Nature, 2021). A genuine revolution.
- Microbiome research
- Synthetic biology
- Aging biology: Hallmarks of Aging papers (Lopez-Otin)

**Key Journals**
- Nature, Science, Cell: the prestige triad
- eLife: open access, high quality
- PLOS Biology: open access
- Molecular Cell, Current Biology, Developmental Cell
- bioRxiv: essential preprint server

**Shadow Curriculum**
- Schrödinger: What is Life? (1944). Argued that genetic 
  material must be an "aperiodic crystal" capable of 
  encoding information, a conceptual framing that influenced 
  Watson and Crick's search for DNA's structure. Note: 
  Schrödinger did not predict DNA specifically; that 
  experimental discovery came from Watson, Crick, and 
  Franklin.
- Monod: Chance and Necessity.
- Mayr: The Growth of Biological Thought.
- Gould: The Structure of Evolutionary Theory.
- Margulis: Symbiotic Planet.

### Earth Sciences and Climate

**Historical**
- Wegener: The Origin of Continents and Oceans (1915). 
  Plate tectonics, initially rejected.
- Arrhenius: 1896 paper predicting CO2 warming.
- Keeling: The Keeling Curve measurements (1958 onwards).

**Key Texts**
- IPCC Assessment Reports: the gold standard synthesis. 
  Always free at ipcc.ch.
- Wallace-Wells: The Uninhabitable Earth (popular)
- Kolbert: The Sixth Extinction (popular, excellent)

**Key Journals**
- Nature Geoscience
- Journal of Climate
- Geophysical Research Letters
- Earth and Planetary Science Letters

**Key Databases**
- NOAA for climate data
- NASA Earthdata
- Global Carbon Project for emissions data
- EarthArXiv for preprints

---

### Cross-Domain Bridges

- Physics to chemistry: quantum chemistry, computational 
  materials science
- Chemistry to biology: biochemistry, chemical biology, 
  pharmacology
- Biology to medicine: translational research, clinical trials
- Physics to earth science: geophysics, atmospheric physics
- Mathematics to all: statistics, dynamical systems, 
  network theory
- Computer science to all: simulation, machine learning 
  applied to drug discovery, protein folding, climate modeling
