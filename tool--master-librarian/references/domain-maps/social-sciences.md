# Social Sciences: Intellectual Topology

### Economics

**Classical Foundations**
- Smith: The Wealth of Nations (1776). 
  Read Books I-III; Book IV is polemic.
- Ricardo: Principles of Political Economy.
- Mill: Principles of Political Economy.
- Marx: Capital Vol. I. Volumes II and III are posthumous 
  and fragmentary.
- Marshall: Principles of Economics.

**20th Century Canon**
- Keynes: The General Theory of Employment, Interest 
  and Money (1936).
- Hayek: The Pure Theory of Capital and Prices and 
  Production.
- Samuelson: Foundations of Economic Analysis.
- Arrow and Debreu: Papers on general equilibrium (1950s).
- Friedman: A Monetary History of the United States.
- Sen: Collective Choice and Social Welfare, then 
  Development as Freedom.

**Behavioral Economics**
- Kahneman and Tversky: "Judgment under Uncertainty: 
  Heuristics and Biases" (1974 Science paper). 
  The founding text.
- Kahneman: Thinking, Fast and Slow (popular)
- Thaler and Sunstein: Nudge
- Ariely: Predictably Irrational (accessible entry)

**Institutional Economics**
- Veblen: The Theory of the Leisure Class
- Coase: "The Nature of the Firm" and "The Problem 
  of Social Cost"
- North: Institutions, Institutional Change and 
  Economic Performance
- Ostrom: Governing the Commons.

**Development Economics**
- Lewis: "Economic Development with Unlimited 
  Supplies of Labour" (1954 paper)
- Hirschman: The Strategy of Economic Development
- Sachs: The End of Poverty
- Easterly: The White Man's Burden
- Banerjee and Duflo: Poor Economics.

**Key Journals**
- American Economic Review
- Quarterly Journal of Economics
- Journal of Political Economy
- Review of Economic Studies
- Journal of Economic Literature: essential for surveys
- NBER Working Papers: nber.org

**Shadow Curriculum**
- Veblen: The Theory of Business Enterprise
- Minsky: Stabilizing an Unstable Economy
- Polanyi: The Great Transformation.
- Hirschman: Exit, Voice and Loyalty
- Galbraith: The New Industrial State
- Streeck: How Will Capitalism End?

### Sociology

**Classical Canon**
- Durkheim: The Division of Labour in Society, then Suicide.
- Weber: The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism, 
  then Economy and Society.
- Simmel: The Sociology of Georg Simmel (selected essays).
- Du Bois: The Philadelphia Negro and The Souls of Black Folk.

**20th Century**
- Parsons: The Social System.
- Merton: Social Theory and Social Structure.
- Goffman: The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life. 
  Then Stigma and Asylums.
- Bourdieu: Distinction, then Outline of a Theory of 
  Practice.
- Foucault: Discipline and Punish.
- Habermas: The Theory of Communicative Action.
- Giddens: The Constitution of Society.
- Luhmann: Social Systems. Very difficult.
- Collins: The Credential Society.
- Tilly: The Contentious French and Durable Inequality.

**Key Journals**
- American Journal of Sociology
- American Sociological Review
- Social Forces
- Theory and Society
- British Journal of Sociology
- Sociology of Education
- Gender and Society

**Shadow Curriculum**
- Elias: The Civilizing Process.
- Berger and Luckmann: The Social Construction of Reality.
- Becker: Outsiders.
- Sennett: The Fall of Public Man and The Corrosion 
  of Character.
- Lamont: The Dignity of Working Men and Money, Morals 
  and Manners.

### Psychology

**Historical**
- James: Principles of Psychology (1890).
- Freud: The Interpretation of Dreams.
- Pavlov: Conditioned Reflexes.
- Skinner: The Behavior of Organisms.
- Piaget: The Origins of Intelligence in Children.

**Cognitive Revolution**
- Miller: "The Magical Number Seven" (1956 paper).
- Neisser: Cognitive Psychology (1967).
- Chomsky: Review of Skinner's Verbal Behavior.

**Social Psychology**
- Milgram: Obedience to Authority.
- Zimbardo: The Lucifer Effect.
- Tajfel and Turner: Social Identity Theory papers.
- Festinger: A Theory of Cognitive Dissonance.
- Asch: Conformity experiments (papers, 1951-1956).

**Clinical**
- DSM-5: the diagnostic manual. Use critically.
- Beck: Cognitive Therapy and the Emotional Disorders
- Linehan: Cognitive-Behavioral Treatment of Borderline 
  Personality Disorder.

**Replication Crisis**
- Open Science Collaboration: "Estimating the 
  Reproducibility of Psychological Science" 
  (Science, 2015). Essential for evaluating any 
  psychology finding.

**Key Journals**
- Psychological Review
- Psychological Bulletin (meta-analyses)
- Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
- Cognition
- Psychological Science
- PsyArXiv: preprints

**Shadow Curriculum**
- Luria: The Mind of a Mnemonist
- Vygotsky: Mind in Society
- Bruner: Acts of Meaning
- Csikszentmihalyi: Flow (original research base)
- Damasio: Descartes' Error

### Anthropology

**Classical**
- Boas: Race, Language and Culture.
- Malinowski: Argonauts of the Western Pacific.
- Radcliffe-Brown: Structure and Function in Primitive 
  Society.

**20th Century**
- Lévi-Strauss: Structural Anthropology and The Savage Mind.
- Turner: The Ritual Process.
- Douglas: Purity and Danger.
- Geertz: The Interpretation of Cultures.
- Sahlins: Stone Age Economics.
- Wolf: Europe and the People Without History.

**Recent**
- Tsing: The Mushroom at the End of the World.
- Graeber: Debt: The First 5000 Years.
- Scott: Seeing Like a State.

**Key Journals**
- American Anthropologist
- American Ethnologist
- Journal of the Royal Anthropological Institute
- Current Anthropology
- Annual Review of Anthropology: essential for surveys

### Political Science

**Classical**
- Thucydides: History of the Peloponnesian War.
- Machiavelli: The Prince and Discourses on Livy.
- Hobbes: Leviathan.
- Locke: Two Treatises of Government.
- Montesquieu: The Spirit of the Laws.
- The Federalist Papers.
- Tocqueville: Democracy in America.

**Modern**
- Dahl: Who Governs?
- Schumpeter: Capitalism, Socialism and Democracy.
- Downs: An Economic Theory of Democracy.
- Olson: The Logic of Collective Action.
- Lipset: Political Man.

**International Relations**
- Morgenthau: Politics Among Nations.
- Waltz: Theory of International Politics.
- Keohane: After Hegemony.
- Wendt: Social Theory of International Politics.
- Mearsheimer: The Tragedy of Great Power Politics.

**Comparative Politics**
- Moore: Social Origins of Dictatorship and Democracy.
- Lijphart: Patterns of Democracy.
- Acemoglu and Robinson: Why Nations Fail.
- Putnam: Making Democracy Work and Bowling Alone.

**Key Journals**
- American Political Science Review
- American Journal of Political Science
- World Politics
- International Organization
- Comparative Political Studies
- Journal of Democracy
