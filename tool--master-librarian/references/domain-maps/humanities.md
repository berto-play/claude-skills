# Humanities: Intellectual Topology

### History

**Philosophy of History**
- Collingwood: The Idea of History
- Carr: What is History?
- White: Metahistory.
- Jenkins: Rethinking History.

**Major Schools and Approaches**

Annales School (France): Long-term structures over events.
- Bloch: Feudal Society and The Historian's Craft
- Braudel: The Mediterranean and the Mediterranean World 
  in the Age of Philip II.
- Le Roy Ladurie: Montaillou.

Social History:
- Thompson: The Making of the English Working Class.
- Hobsbawm: The Age of Revolution, Age of Capital, Age of 
  Empire, Age of Extremes (four-volume 19th-20th century 
  history).
- Zinn: A People's History of the United States.

Cultural History:
- Burckhardt: The Civilization of the Renaissance in Italy.
- Huizinga: The Waning of the Middle Ages.
- Ginzburg: The Cheese and the Worms.
- Darnton: The Great Cat Massacre.
- Davis: The Return of Martin Guerre.

Postcolonial History:
- Fanon: The Wretched of the Earth.
- Said: Orientalism.
- Chakrabarty: Provincializing Europe.
- Subaltern Studies collective.

**Key Journals**
- American Historical Review
- Past and Present (UK)
- Journal of Modern History
- Journal of Social History
- History and Theory
- JSTOR is the essential database.

**Shadow Curriculum**
- Elias: The Civilizing Process
- Mann: The Sources of Social Power (4 vols)
- McNeill: Plagues and Peoples
- Crosby: Ecological Imperialism

### Literature and Literary Theory

**Ancient**
- Homer: Iliad and Odyssey. Read Fagles or Wilson 
  translations.
- Greek tragedy: Aeschylus, Sophocles, Euripides. 
  Read all three.
- Virgil: Aeneid.
- Ovid: Metamorphoses.
- Dante: Divine Comedy. Mandelbaum translation.
- Chaucer: Canterbury Tales.

**Literary Theory**
- Aristotle: Poetics.

Russian Formalism:
- Shklovsky: "Art as Technique" (essay)
- Propp: Morphology of the Folktale

New Criticism:
- Brooks: The Well Wrought Urn
- Wimsatt and Beardsley: "The Intentional Fallacy" and 
  "The Affective Fallacy"

Structuralism:
- Saussure: Course in General Linguistics.
- Barthes: Mythologies and S/Z.
- Genette: Narrative Discourse.

Poststructuralism:
- Derrida: Of Grammatology
- Barthes: "The Death of the Author" (essay, 1967)
- Foucault: "What is an Author?" (essay, 1969)
- de Man: Allegories of Reading

Feminist Criticism:
- Woolf: A Room of One's Own
- Gilbert and Gubar: The Madwoman in the Attic
- Showalter: A Literature of Their Own

Postcolonial Criticism:
- Said: Culture and Imperialism
- Bhabha: The Location of Culture
- Spivak: "Can the Subaltern Speak?" (essay)

Queer Theory:
- Sedgwick: Epistemology of the Closet
- Butler: Gender Trouble

**Key Journals**
- PMLA: broad
- Critical Inquiry: theory-focused
- New Literary History
- ELH (English Literary History)
- Representations: cultural poetics
- MLA International Bibliography: essential database

### Linguistics

**Foundational**
- Saussure: Course in General Linguistics (1916).
- Bloomfield: Language.
- Jakobson: Selected Writings.

**Generative Linguistics**
- Chomsky: Syntactic Structures (1957) and Aspects of 
  the Theory of Syntax (1965).
- Pinker: The Language Instinct (accessible)

**Cognitive Linguistics**
- Lakoff and Johnson: Metaphors We Live By.
- Lakoff: Women, Fire and Dangerous Things.
- Langacker: Foundations of Cognitive Grammar.

**Sociolinguistics**
- Labov: Sociolinguistic Patterns.
- Trudgill: Dialects.

**Pragmatics**
- Austin: How to Do Things with Words.
- Grice: "Logic and Conversation" (essay).
- Sperber and Wilson: Relevance.

**Historical Linguistics**
- Campbell: Historical Linguistics.
- Fortson: Indo-European Language and Culture.

**Key Journals**
- Language (Linguistic Society of America)
- Linguistics
- Cognitive Linguistics
- Language in Society

### Art History and Visual Studies

**Foundational**
- Wölfflin: Principles of Art History.
- Panofsky: Studies in Iconology.
- Gombrich: The Story of Art (accessible) and Art and 
  Illusion (scholarly).
- Berger: Ways of Seeing.

**Social History of Art**
- Clark: The Painting of Modern Life.
- Nochlin: "Why Have There Been No Great Women Artists?" 
  (essay, 1971)
- Baxandall: Painting and Experience in Fifteenth Century 
  Italy.

**Critical Theory and Visual Culture**
- Benjamin: "The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical 
  Reproduction" (essay, 1935).
- Debord: The Society of the Spectacle.
- Mulvey: "Visual Pleasure and Narrative Cinema" 
  (essay, 1975).
- Mitchell: Picture Theory.

**Architecture**
- Vitruvius: Ten Books on Architecture.
- Venturi: Complexity and Contradiction in Architecture.
- Jacobs: The Death and Life of Great American Cities.
- Koolhaas: Delirious New York.

**Key Journals**
- The Art Bulletin
- October (theory-focused)
- Burlington Magazine
- Journal of the Society of Architectural Historians
- Grove Art Online: the essential encyclopedia
