## Dark Mode Adaptation Rules

When generating a dark variant from any light palette:

1. **Background**: Not pure black. Use the accent hue at 5-10% lightness (e.g., blue accent → `hsl(220, 20%, 7%)`).
2. **Surface**: 3-5% lighter than background. Each elevation step adds 2-3%.
3. **Foreground**: Not pure white. Use 90-95% lightness.
4. **Accent**: Desaturate 10-20% and increase lightness 10-15% from the light-mode accent.
5. **Borders**: Visible but subtle. 8-15% lightness.
6. **Shadows**: Near-invisible on dark backgrounds. Use border or subtle glow instead.
7. **Semantic colors**: Keep hue, reduce saturation, adjust lightness for readability on dark.
