# Job Sources Reference

Indexed sources for job market intelligence. Ordered by reliability for freshness
verification. Always cross-reference at least two sources per role.

---

## Tier 1: Primary Sources (highest freshness reliability)

### Company Careers Page (direct)
The most authoritative source. Posting date on the careers page reflects when the
company actually listed the role, not when a third-party board scraped it.

**How to find:** Google "[company name] careers" or "[company name] jobs". Most
companies use a path like /careers, /jobs, or /work-with-us.

**ATS portals to recognise:**
- Greenhouse: `boards.greenhouse.io/[company]`
- Lever: `jobs.lever.co/[company]`
- Workday: `[company].wd*.myworkdayjobs.com`
- Ashby: `jobs.ashbyhq.com/[company]`
- Recruitee: `[company].recruitee.com`
- Teamtailor: `[company].teamtailor.com`

When you identify the ATS, you can often browse all open roles for the company
in one place, sorted by date.

**Freshness signal:** date shown on the individual role page. Trust this above
any third-party board date.

---

### LinkedIn Jobs
Most comprehensive single source for European and global job market. Has the
largest volume of postings and the most reliable "posted X days ago" signals.

**Search URL pattern:** linkedin.com/jobs/search — filter by: keyword, location,
date posted (past 24h / past week / past month), experience level.

**Freshness signals:**
- "Posted X hours ago" = highly fresh
- "Posted X days ago" = fresh if <14 days
- "Be an early applicant" tag = usually <48 hours old
- "Actively recruiting" badge = hiring manager is responsive

**Watch out for:** "Promoted" posts — these are paid placements and may be
evergreen roles kept visible artificially. Cross-check with careers page.

**Hiring signal intelligence:** LinkedIn company page shows headcount and growth
chart (visible without login for most companies). Compare to post volume.

## Tier 2: Strong Secondary Sources

### Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent)
Best source for startups, particularly Series A-C. Most roles are posted directly
by founders or hiring managers — lower phantom rate than large boards.

**URL:** wellfound.com/jobs  
**Filters:** role type, location, remote, company stage, salary range  
**Freshness:** generally reliable; most startups update listings actively  
**Unique value:** shows equity, salary transparency, and direct access to founders

### Otta
Curated job board focused on tech and startup roles. Smaller volume but lower
noise. Good for London, Europe, and remote roles.

**URL:** otta.com  
**Strength:** roles are manually reviewed; phantom rate is lower than aggregators  
**Freshness:** shows date posted reliably

### Glassdoor Jobs
Large aggregator with good European coverage. Also useful for company reviews,
salary data, and interview process intelligence.

**URL:** glassdoor.com/Job  
**Freshness signal:** less reliable than LinkedIn — many posts are syndicated  
**Unique value:** "Interview Reviews" section — search by company to find real
candidate experiences and typical process timelines

---

## Tier 3: Aggregators (use for volume, verify elsewhere)

### Indeed
Largest global aggregator. High volume, but freshness signals are unreliable
because posts are scraped and re-indexed continuously.

**URL:** indeed.com (or indeed.it, indeed.co.uk, etc. for local)  
**Use for:** finding roles you might miss elsewhere; verifying a role is still live  
**Freshness caveat:** "Posted X days ago" on Indeed often reflects the scrape date,
not the original post date. Always verify against careers page.

### Google Jobs
Google indexes job posts from most boards and company pages. Useful for a broad
sweep but not for freshness verification.

**Search technique:** Google "[role title] [location] jobs" — Jobs panel appears
at the top. Filter by date posted (past week / past month).

**Freshness caveat:** Same as Indeed — scrape date, not post date. Verify elsewhere.

## EU Remote Verification Sources

Use these specifically to confirm whether a US company will hire EU-based employees.

### LinkedIn Company "People" tab
Filter employees by location. If the company already has employees in Italy, Germany,
France, Spain, or other EU countries, EU hiring is confirmed by practice.
Search: company page → People tab → filter by Country/Region → select EU countries.

### Glassdoor Reviews (filter by location)
Filter reviews by reviewer location. EU-based employee reviews confirm the company
hires in Europe. Look for mentions of "Remote EU", "hired from [EU country]", or
EOR/contractor arrangements.

### Deel / Remote.com / Rippling Global customer lists
These Employer of Record platforms publish customer or case study lists. If a company
appears there, they have the infrastructure to hire globally.
Search: "[company name] Deel" or "[company name] Remote.com" or "[company name] employer of record".

### Job description keywords to search
When reading a US remote job post, search the description for:
- "EU", "Europe", "EMEA", "CET", "GMT", "timezone agnostic", "globally distributed",
  "work from anywhere", "Deel", "Remote.com", "employer of record", "contractor OK"

Any of these is a positive signal. "US only", "must be authorized to work in the US",
"we cannot sponsor visas" are disqualifying signals (even for remote — visa language
often signals US-only hiring infrastructure).

### Levels.fyi and Blind (for tech roles)
Community forums where employees discuss remote policies. Search "[company] remote EU"
or "[company] hire Europe" for real employee experiences.

## EU-Wide Job Sources

### EuropeRemotely
europeremotely.com — curated remote roles specifically for EU-based candidates.
All listed roles have been verified as EU-remote eligible. High signal, lower volume.

### Remote OK (EU filter)
remoteok.com — global remote job board. Filter by timezone (Europe) to surface
roles where EU overlap is expected. Does not verify EU employment legality —
still apply the EU Remote Filter.

### We Work Remotely
weworkremotely.com — established remote board. Skews US companies but many
explicitly allow EU hires. Read job description carefully for EU signals.

### Jobgether
jobgether.com — European-focused remote job board. Strong for tech, product,
and design. Good freshness signals.

### Remote First
remotefirst.com — curated remote roles at companies with explicit remote-first
culture. Higher probability of EU acceptance.

### Himalayas
himalayas.app — remote job board with transparency on location requirements.
Many listings explicitly state accepted countries or timezones.

## Italy-Specific Sources

### LinkedIn Italia
linkedin.com/jobs — filter location to Italy. Most reliable for Italian market.
Use Italian job titles alongside English (e.g. "Product Designer" and "Designer
di Prodotto") — Italian companies often post in Italian.

### InfoJobs Italia
infojobs.it — major Italian job board. Higher volume for traditional roles.
Less useful for startup/tech market.

### Talent.io
talent.io — European tech job board with strong Italian startup presence.
Developer and product-focused. Salary transparency.

### Substack / Beehiiv newsletters
Several Italian VC and startup newsletters post open roles from portfolio companies.
Useful for finding roles before they hit the major boards. Examples: newsletters
from VC funds active in Italian tech (P101, Primo Ventures, United Ventures).

## Startup-Specific Sources

### Y Combinator Jobs
ycombinator.com/jobs — roles at YC-backed companies. Strong signal for well-funded,
operationally serious startups. Freshness generally reliable.

### Sequoia / a16z / Index portfolio job boards
Most major VC funds maintain a portfolio jobs page. Find it by Googling "[VC name]
portfolio jobs". Roles here are at funded, vetted companies.

### Crunchbase
crunchbase.com — not a job board, but shows recent funding rounds. Companies that
closed a Series A-B in the last 6 months are in active hiring mode. Cross-reference
with their careers page.

## Hiring Signal Intelligence Sources

### LinkedIn Company Page
Shows: headcount, headcount growth chart (6-month and 2-year), active job count,
recent posts, new hire announcements. All visible without login.

**How to read headcount growth:** if headcount grew 20%+ in the last 6 months and
active job count is high, the company is in genuine growth mode.

### Glassdoor Company Profile
Shows: rating trends, CEO approval, recent reviews, interview reviews.
A company with declining ratings and increasing job posts may be in churn.

### Crunchbase / Dealroom
Shows: funding history, last funding date, total raised, investor roster.
A company that raised 12+ months ago with no new round may have slowing runway.
A company that raised <6 months ago is likely in active hiring.

### LinkedIn "People" tab on company page
Shows recent hires by title and tenure. Cross-reference with open roles to detect
phantom posts (if someone just joined in the target function, the role may be filled).

## Search Technique Notes

**Boolean search on LinkedIn:** use quotes for exact titles ("Senior Product Designer"),
AND/OR for alternatives ("Product Designer" OR "UX Designer"), NOT to exclude
("Design NOT graphic").

**Italian language search:** always run a second search in Italian for Italian
companies. "Designer di Prodotto Senior", "Lead UX", "Responsabile Prodotto".
Some roles are only posted in Italian.

**"Easy Apply" filter:** on LinkedIn, Easy Apply means the company is using
LinkedIn's native application flow. This can indicate a high-volume, lower-signal
search. Roles requiring an external application (direct to ATS) often indicate
a more deliberate hiring process.

**Remote filter nuance:** "Remote" on LinkedIn in Europe often means "Remote in
[specific country]" due to tax and employment law. Always check the job description
for the actual remote policy. "Remote EU" vs. "Remote Italy" vs. "Hybrid Milan"
are meaningfully different.

## Tensor Scoring Guidance Per Source Type

Apply this when assigning the Source Confidence Tensor flags [P][C][I][F][E] to each role.

### PRIMARY flag (P)

| Source | P flag |
|---|---|
| Company careers page (direct URL) | P=1 — this is the definition of PRIMARY |
| ATS portals (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, Recruitee, Teamtailor) when accessed via the company's own board URL | P=1 — same as careers page |
| LinkedIn Jobs, Wellfound, Otta, Glassdoor, Indeed, Google Jobs | P=0 — these are SECONDARY sources; they can contribute to CORROBORATED but not PRIMARY |
| Agency listings, recruiter job posts | P=0 — agency posts never qualify as PRIMARY |

**Rule:** A role with P=0 is not in the active ledger. Find the company careers page before reporting the role as active.

### CORROBORATED flag (C)

| Situation | C flag |
|---|---|
| Same role confirmed on careers page AND at least one job board | C=1 |
| Same role confirmed on 2+ job boards that are not the same feed | C=1 |
| Same role found only on careers page (no board cross-reference) | C=0 — acceptable if P=1 and F=1 |
| Same role found only on 1 job board | C=0 |

### INDEPENDENT flag (I)

| Situation | I flag |
|---|---|
| Careers page + LinkedIn Jobs (LinkedIn scrapes independently) | I=1 |
| Careers page + Wellfound (Wellfound operates independently) | I=1 |
| LinkedIn Jobs + Indeed (both aggregate from many sources) | I=1 — usually independent pipelines |
| Indeed + ZipRecruiter + SimplyHired (these share feeds frequently) | I=0 — likely same syndication pipeline |
| 2+ boards showing identical JD text with different dates | I=0 — syndication ghost, not independent |
| Agency listing + original employer post | I=0 — agency listing derives from employer source |

**Rule:** When in doubt, check if the JD text is word-for-word identical across boards.
Identical text = syndicated = I=0.

### FRESH flag (F)

| Signal | F flag |
|---|---|
| Visible date on careers page or board within the operator's freshness window | F=1 |
| No date visible on any source | F=0 — exclude from active ledger |
| Date outside the freshness window | F=0 — move to Stale/Flagged section |
| Date visible only on a low-trust aggregator (Indeed, ZipRecruiter) | F=0 unless corroborated by a higher-trust source with the same date |

**Rule:** If F=0, the role goes to Stale/Flagged, not the active ledger. No exceptions.

### EU-VERIFIED flag (E)

| Signal | E flag |
|---|---|
| Hard EU signal present (see Part A of SKILL.md for full list) | E=1 |
| Soft signal only (timezone mention, "remote-friendly" without EU specifics) | E=0 |
| No EU signal of any kind | E=0 |
| Role is at an EU-based company | E=1 by default (EU companies hire EU employees) |

**Rule:** For US company roles, E=0 means the role goes to EU Eligibility Unconfirmed,
not the active ledger. E cannot be assumed — it must be verified.
